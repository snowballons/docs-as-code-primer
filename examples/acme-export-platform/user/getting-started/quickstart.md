# Quickstart: Export your data

Get your first export in a few minutes.

## Before you start

- An enterprise admin account
- Permission: **Data export**

## Steps

1. Sign in to Acme.
2. Open **Data → Export**.
3. Choose a date range (up to 24 months) and format (CSV, JSON, or XLSX).
4. Click **Start export**.
5. When the export is ready, open the email link or download from **Export history**.

## What success looks like

You have a complete file that opens in your spreadsheet or tools without corruption errors.
